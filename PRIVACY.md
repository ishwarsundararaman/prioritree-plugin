# PrioriTree Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

This policy explains what information PrioriTree ("PrioriTree", "we", "us") handles when you use the
PrioriTree plugin, desktop app and any related online services (together, the "Service"), and how that
information is used. We'll update it as the Service changes; see "Changes to this policy" below.

## Summary

- **The plugin and desk run on your computer.** They read your AI chat history from your device to build
  your work map. The map is stored on your device.
- **Online features are optional.** These include a personal link, syncing to other devices and
  sharing. If you turn one on, a copy of the information needed for that feature is stored on servers
  we operate or use.
- **Your AI provider processes your chats under your agreement with them.** When your own AI assistant
  (for example Codex or Claude) reads your chats to build or update the map, that processing happens
  inside your account with that provider. Their privacy terms apply to it.
- **We don't sell your personal information.**

## Information we handle

**On your device (local features).** To build your work map, the Service reads:

- your AI chat history from supported apps installed on your computer, such as the Codex app;
- basic project information from those apps.

From this it creates and stores the following on your device:

- your work map: businesses, goals, initiatives, steps, status, and your decisions and corrections;
- a history of changes to the map;
- counts of your own messages, used to show where your effort went.

**If you use online features.** When you choose to use a feature that works online, we may collect
and store:

- **Account information:** for example your name, email address and the sign-in provider you use,
  such as Google.
- **Map content:** a copy of your work map and related history, so it can be shown on your other
  devices or to the people you share it with. We design online features so that your raw chat
  transcripts don't need to leave your device. Some text from your chats, such as titles, short
  evidence notes or your corrections, may form part of the map.
- **Usage and device information:** for example when you open the Service, which features you use,
  the app version, device type and error reports. We use this to run, secure and improve the Service.

**When you contact us,** we receive the information you send, such as your email address and message.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- provide, maintain and improve the Service;
- show your map to you and to the people you choose to share it with;
- understand how the Service is used;
- keep the Service secure and prevent misuse;
- communicate with you about the Service;
- comply with legal obligations.

## How information is shared

We share information only in these cases:

- **Service providers** that help us operate the Service, such as hosting, storage, sign-in and
  error reporting. They act on our instructions.
- **People you choose to share with,** for example through a share link.
- **Your AI provider,** as described above, when you ask your own AI assistant to read or update
  your map.
- **Legal and safety reasons,** where required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security
  of users, the public or PrioriTree.
- **Business transfers,** for example if PrioriTree is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of
  assets. Information may then be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

## Storage and retention

- **Local data** stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it by removing the
  PrioriTree data folder. On Windows this is `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PrioriTree`.
- **Online data** is kept while your account or the relevant feature is active. You can ask us to
  delete your account and online data at any time. We'll then delete it within a reasonable period,
  except where we need to keep some information to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or
  enforce our agreements. Copies in routine backups may persist for a limited time before they are
  overwritten.

Online data may be stored and processed in countries other than your own, using service providers
that offer appropriate protections.

## Your choices and rights

- **You decide whether to use online features.** The local plugin and desk work without them.
- **You can turn off start-at-login, disconnect a device or stop sharing** at any time.
- **Depending on where you live,** you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of
  your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us using
  the details below.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we handle. No method of storage or
transmission is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

## Children

The Service is not intended for people under 18. We don't knowingly collect personal information
from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the updated version with a new "Last
updated" date. If we make material changes, we'll take reasonable steps to let you know, for example
in the app, on our website or by email.
